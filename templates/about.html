About Our Project

Empowering Students for a Better Career Future

AI-Based Resume Analyzer & Skill Gap Analyzer

Our project is designed to help students analyze their resumes, identify their existing skills and discover the skills required for their desired job roles.

Our Objectives

  • Analyze uploaded resumes.
  • Identify technical and professional skills.
  • Calculate job role skill matching percentage.
  • Identify missing skills.
  • Provide learning recommendations.

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Flask Framework
  • HTML and CSS
  • SQLite Database
  • PyMuPDF

Our Goal

Our goal is to bridge the gap between students' existing skills and industry requirements by providing useful resume analysis and career guidance.
